## 7. Risks
1. **Phone access.** Every device row is blocked while the A16 stays locked; S24+ never measured. Mitigation: owner unlocks before the session; the script rejects locked runs rather than guess.
2. **Game lacks launch args** (T2) and the importer does not load `gate3_cameras.json` (T3): G3-B and 3/4 shots cannot be taken on the phone; the script reports this per camera instead of faking frames.
3. **Budget misses.** Estimates already exceed Medium at Rondo Putry (653 k tris vs 550 k) and High forest at DK8 (8.2 M) is outside the segments but streaming/LOD policy is shared; fixes may cost visual density (anti-slop vs FPS). Do not turn off the mirror for a pretty benchmark.
4. **World size.** Low world estimate 55.6 MiB vs 60 MB budget is UNVERIFIED; High content is 400 MB of GLB and not shippable as is.
5. **Data confidence.** Ramp lanes/speed `low_confidence`, hall colour, gate layout, gatehouse position unverified; routes through ramps depend on the OSM link 13 m off the carriageway (map-decisions C). Risk of polishing geometry that photos later contradict.
6. **Heights.** Camera U and the autopilot's y come from bare-earth NMT / raycast; any structure (Putry ramp) can sit higher. Eye cameras may end up under a deck until re-seated.
7. **Reference quality.** No real owner photos; CC0 set shows other angles and seasons (May, November, December). Side-by-side is "same elements", not pixel-matched frames.
8. **Process.** One Unity process per project: builds/visual-qa/tests must be queued by the lead; the run script starts Unity for the build.
9. **Subjective gate.** Owner's art-direction acceptance cannot be automated; define the "no" path (what changes if the direction is rejected) before the session.
