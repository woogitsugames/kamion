**Werdykt ogólny: dalej FAIL na bramce `docs/06` §5 we wszystkich czterech obszarach, ale postęp jest duży i realny.** Główny powód FAIL jest wspólny: nadal **nie ma ani jednego kadru URP lub z telefonu z nowymi zasobami** (A4, A6 i świat v2 istnieją tylko w Blenderze). Pierwsze zrzuty z telefonu pokazują jeszcze graybox i świat v1. Nie ma też prawdziwych zdjęć: obrazy w `Data/photos/` są wygenerowane przez AI, więc pomagają, ale nie zaliczają punktu 3.

- **Wnętrze A6: najbliżej PASS (≈7/10).** Zegary są już całe w oknie kierownicy (sprawdziłem na obrazie, test linii wzroku działa). Kierownica, fotele i półka pasażera nie wyglądają już jak klocki. Do poprawy: (1) w kadrach „URP-like” na dole szyby świeci **biały pas** (prawdopodobnie zastępcza karoseria widoczna przez szybę, bez czarnego rantu), (2) przy niskim słońcu wnętrze w modelu gry jest prawie czarne, więc potrzebne jest światło kabiny lub sonda (decyzja `rendering-perf`), (3) rendery kontrolne trzeba powtórzyć w wybranym **koniaku**, bo obecne są w grafitowym.
- **Zewnętrze A4 TRAKT: FAIL (≈5/10).** Wymiary, podwozie, koła, opony, siodło i przyłącza są bardzo dobre. Ale przód z trzema polami poziomych listew i reflektorami z „klocków LED” czyta się jak **ciężarówka z ok. 2000 r. albo z gry mobilnej z 2016**, nie z lat 2020. Dolna 1/3 to ciemny „cokół” (zderzak + nadkole + stopnie), na którym kabina stoi jak na podstawce. Stopnie są w czarnej wnęce **za drzwiami**, a nie pod nimi.
- **Pytanie o punkt oczu i drzwi:** **nie przesuwajcie punktu oczu.** Oczy 0,45 m przed osią są wiarygodne przy kabinie kończącej się 0,95 m za osią: przesunięcie oczu o 0,3 m do tyłu zabrałoby miejsce na leżankę. Przesuńcie **otwór drzwi** o ok. 0,3 m do tyłu (tył drzwi ≈ −0,6 m), z przodu zostawcie stały panel narożny. Dolny tylny róg drzwi niech schodzi za nadkolem i przykrywa najwyższy stopień. Stopnie o szerokości ~0,55–0,6 m są typowe; w R1 źle oceniłem, że muszą mieć szerokość drzwi. Potwierdźcie to Waszym zdjęciem z miarką: oś koła → tylna krawędź drzwi, szerokość stopni, wysokość progu.
- **Świat v2: FAIL, ale skok jakości jest ogromny (z ≈3/10 na ≈5/10).** Drzewa nie są już lizakami, są place, ogrodzenia, ekrany akustyczne, bariery i rampy (rampy są naprawdę dobre). Teraz widać nowe problemy: **budynki to gołe pudełka z oknami** (dominują w kadrze i wyglądają jak gra z 2012), puste place bez aut i naczep, brama bez portierni (szlaban to biała rurka), proste „narysowane linijką” spękania asfaltu, łaty w regularnej siatce, **niebieskie cienie przy niskim słońcu** (efekt teal-orange, zakazany), sosny podobne do palm lub cedrów.
- **Telefon (pierwsze zrzuty): FAIL wizualnie, ale bardzo cenne.** 30 FPS trzymane, lusterka działają, nakładanie przycisków naprawione, font poprawiony. Ale obraz to „Unity z 2016”: nasycone niebieskie niebo, płaska zielona trawa bez tekstury, brak cieni i mgły, tekst diagnostyczny na środku widoku. Zrzuty są zmniejszone do 900×415, więc nie da się ocenić aliasingu.
- **Uwaga do `docs/REFERENCES.md`:** plik mówi „stopnie przed przednim kołem, pod drzwiami”. Oba obrazy AI z ciężarówką pokazują stopnie **za** kołem, pod tylną częścią drzwi. To trzeba poprawić, bo inaczej agent zbuduje błędną geometrię.

## 1. A3/A4: TRAKT exterior, concept B (owner: `truck-artist`)

**Verdict: FAIL.** Scores (1–10): scale/proportion 8 · silhouette/design era 4 · materials 6 · detail density 7 (chassis 8, front 4) · wear/embedding 3 · EU/PL realism 6 · readability 7 · artefacts 7.

### What A4 must keep
- Measured, legal dimensions (2,548 m body width, 3,98 m height, 6,06 m length) and the automated `wymiary.json`/`stats.json`.
- Chassis behind the cab: aluminium tank with straps, AdBlue with blue cap, catwalk with chequer plate, coils on a gooseneck with correct red/yellow air and black electrical, fifth wheel with lock and lever, rear wings, lamps, plate. This is the most believable part of the truck (`zbl_tyl_*`), compared with the AI chassis sheet.
- Wheels and tyres: forged rim with a deep dish on the rear duals, tread, and non-branded sidewall text with plausible markings (156/150L, 3PMSF).
- The visibility map and ray-cast tooling (`widocznosc_mapa.png`, `top.jpg`): A-pillar 8°/3.5°, ground visible from ~5 m. This is exemplary engineering QA. Class V/VI mirrors are present.
- The tilt test, LOD plan, 6 game material slots, ~99.8 k tris.

### Defects (ranked)
1. **P0, door/step/arch relation (answer to the open question).** In `bok_white_overcast.jpg` the door ends at the tilt line (z 1,22 m), its rear edge is at y −0,28 m, and the 3-step stack sits in a black recessed box *behind* the door (x≈415–445 px at 900 px). The driver would climb a ladder beside the door, not into it. In real cab-overs (and both AI truck sheets, AI reference, not photo) the front wheel sits under the front half of the door. The steps sit behind the wheel, **under the rear half of the door**, and the door's lower rear corner drops down as a skirt over the top step.
   **Fix:** keep `driver_eye_point_m` (0,45 m ahead of the axle). It is consistent with a cab back at −0,95 m and a bunk of at least 0,75 m. Moving it 0,3 m rearward would starve the bunk. Instead:
   - Move the **door opening** rearward by ~0,3 m (rear edge ≈ −0,60 m, front edge ≈ +0,80 m).
   - Add a **fixed body-colour corner panel** ahead of the door, carrying the corner deflector.
   - Add a door skirt down to ≈0,95 m behind the arch, with body-colour step-well panels instead of black boxes, and 3 steps 0,55–0,6 m wide. My R1 demand for "steps the width of the door" was wrong; 0,55–0,6 m is normal for the class.
   - Re-run `widocznosc`: the main mirror is seen through the door glass. Update the A5/A6 door card opening.
   - Confirm with an owner photo and a tape: hub centre → door rear edge, step widths, sill height.
   Owner: `truck-artist`, plus the interior door card.
2. **P0, the front reads as ~2000, not 2020s** (`przod_*`, `34przod_*`, `zbl_reflektor_*`).
   - There are three stacked fields of identical horizontal slats: the upper grille, the bumper intake and the lower intake, all with the same pitch and bevel. This hits the docs/06 "identical bevels" flag.
   - The headlamp is a rectangular box with 5 white cubes, 2 chrome balls and an orange bar behind flat, invisible glass. It reads as an aftermarket LED kit or LEGO, not an OEM lamp. There is no outer lens with thickness and reflections, no inner bezel or reflector depth, and no projector shroud. The DRL "slash" sits on top of the housing edge.
   - Real 2020s fronts (AI sheet, AI reference, not photo) have one deep grille in a dark frame with 2–3 bar weights, slim lamps under a thick clear lens with inner chrome/black graphics, and a sculpted bumper with air curtains.
   **Fix:**
   - Use one grille field with varied bar depth: the top bars in body colour or dark chrome, a mesh below.
   - Give the bumper intake a different texture (honeycomb or mesh, not slats).
   - Model a lamp lens shell (glass with thickness and a Fresnel highlight) and a reflector bowl behind each projector. Make the LED modules a lit strip inside a black mask, not cubes.
   - Keep the T idea, but let it live in the **grille frame and the DRL**, not in three slat panels.
3. **P1, the lower third reads as a dark plinth.** In `34przod_*` the bumper, the arch shroud and the step box form one continuous dark-grey block (~0,35–1,2 m), and the white cab sits on it like a model on a stand. The white corner panel ends with a hard vertical edge just ahead of the arch (`zbl_stopnie_*`, x≈160). Modern EU cabs carry body colour down to ≈0,9 m around the arch and steps, with black only on the arch lip, the lower bumper and the skirts. **Fix:** body-colour step and corner panels; keep dark only for the arch flare (≈60–80 mm), the lower bumper and the side skirt.
4. **P1, missing front mud flap and spray suppression** behind the front wheel (`zbl_stopnie_*`). It is EU-mandatory and a strong visual cue. Add a rubber flap and a brush strip at the rear of the arch.
5. **P1, too clean.** The "subtle dust" is not readable at 1600 px. The frame, steps, arch and tank are showroom-clean. The fifth wheel grease is flat light grey; it should be dark brown-black with a sheen. Even a fleet truck has a road-film gradient on the lower 0,6 m, rear-wheel spray on the cab back wall, brake dust on the rims, and worn tread on the step plates. The wear masks are planned for A6: bring them forward for the next review frames.
6. **P1, the rims look like chrome.** The polished aluminium (`zbl_kolo_*`) hits the "zbyt błyszczący metal" flag. Raise roughness to ~0.2–0.3, add a radial brushed anisotropy normal and brake dust in the dish.
7. **P1, the mirror heads are seen edge-on from the eye point** (`oko_lewo.jpg`, `oko_prawo.jpg`). From the seat, the main mirror reads as a sliver about 3° wide. In the AI interior sheet (AI reference, not photo) the glass faces the driver and fills ~8–10°. Aim each mirror normal at `EYE_POINT` (R46 adjustment) and check the useful field in the visibility map.
8. **P2, the roof "beak".** A dark fin or wedge sits at the centre of the windscreen header (`34przod_*`, `przod_*`) and reads like a spoiler from a 90s tuning catalogue. Replace it with a flush header with marker lamps, or with a full-width sun visor.
9. **P2, dark livery:** the TRAKT wordmark disappears on bottle green (`34przod_dark_*`). Use a satin-silver wordmark on the dark livery.
10. **P2, the tilt test shows an empty frame** (`tilt.jpg`): no engine, no radiator, no intake. The 50° clearance claim is therefore untested against the engine bay. Add a proxy engine and cooling pack.
11. **P2, render background:** the sawtooth "mountain" forest silhouette is still there in A4 (`34tyl_*`, `zbl_kolo_*`). A6 already has a much better pine/birch card forest; reuse it.
12. **[PIPELINE] P1:** the "URP-like" pass is still Cycles. The first true URP frame comes with the A7 import. Until then, no exterior material can be declared final.

## 2. A6: cab interior (owner: `truck-artist`; lighting: `rendering-perf`)

**Verdict: FAIL (near-miss). This is the best asset in the repo.** Scores: scale/ergonomics 8 · lighting 7 (Cycles), 6 (URP-like overcast), 3 (URP-like low sun) · materials 7 · detail where the driver looks 8 · wear 4 · EU realism 8 · readability 8 · artefacts 6.

### Fixed since R1 (verified on the images)
- **Rim vs cluster** (`kierowca_*`, `zegary_*`): the whole 12.3" cluster sits in the upper rim window.
  - It shows one speed readout, rpm with a green band, both air circuits (10,4/10,2 bar), fuel, AdBlue, AMT "A 12 E", CC/LIM and the odometer.
  - The content is plausible (28,4 l/100 km for 40 t).
  - The sightline test is the right automation.
- **Steering wheel** (`kierownica_*`): broad flat spokes with integrated pods, flush keys, and a firm hub with a crisp edge.
- **Passenger dash** (`pasazer_*`): trays with mats, a speaker grille, split lines and a holder. The flat slab is gone.
- **Mid-tones:** the lower dash, A-pillar and door card are now readable in URP-like overcast. The cab no longer turns into a black hole.
- **Seats:** crowned cushions, a headrest, a belt and a back pocket.

### Defects (ranked)
1. **P0 [PIPELINE], low sun turns the cab black in the game lighting model** (`cycles_vs_urp_pasazer_lowsun.jpg`, right half).
   - Everything outside the sun patch is near-black. This is the most-seen frame at dawn and dusk.
   - **Fix (`rendering-perf`):** treat `T_Cab_AO_*` as baked cab irradiance and multiply it by a per-frame cab ambient term taken from sky luminance (not from the sun). Add one non-shadowing cab fill light, or an in-cab light probe set updated per time of day.
   - **Acceptance:** lower dash and door card above 8% display value at 9° sun.
2. **P1, bright white band at the windscreen base in the URP-like renders.**
   - Where: `kierowca_overcast_urp.jpg` (y≈355–365 at 900 px), `zegary_overcast_urp.jpg` (y≈215–235), lower right of `pasazer_overcast_urp.jpg`.
   - A thin, near-white strip runs the full width between the dash top and the glass. In Cycles it is grey.
   - Most likely cause: `CAB_ShellPlaceholder` (white paint) is seen through the glass without a black frit/sealant band, or the cowl ignores the cab AO. A real windscreen has a 60–120 mm black ceramic frit along the bottom.
   - **Fix:** add a frit band to the glass shader mask and correct the occlusion on the cowl. In the game this strip would read as a light leak.
3. **P1, re-render the control set in the chosen "koniak" trim.**
   - All control frames are still graphite. Koniak changes the value structure (door card, seat centres), and it is what the next review and the URP import must see.
   - The AI interior sheet (AI reference, not photo) supports a lighter overall key: light-grey headliner and bunk, mid-grey dash, a lighter door-card band.
   - Our cab still sits about 1 stop darker on the upper dash and the door uppers.
4. **P1, mirror glass renders as flat white** (`pasazer_overcast*.jpg`, upper left). It reads as an unlit card. Until the render texture exists, use a placeholder reflection (a blurred environment probe) so the control frames do not show white slabs.
5. **P1, stitching decals glow in URP-like.**
   - The seat stitching reads as bright dotted lines in `pasazer_overcast_urp.jpg` (seat, x≈30–230 px). In Cycles it is invisible.
   - The README says this is fixed for belt and stitching, but it still shows on the seat.
   - Check the AO inheritance of the decal atlas on the seat insert.
6. **P1, no warning-light row on the cluster.**
   - A real cluster always shows a row of warning lights: parking brake, ABS/EBS trailer, retarder, low air, preheat, engine fault (MIL). Only a beam icon is present.
   - Add a row of unlit grey warning lights, with 1–2 lit depending on the vehicle state. It is cheap and a strong realism cue.
7. **P2, the windscreen shows no faint reflection of the dash, and no wipers are in view** (`kierowca_*`).
   - Still open from R1.
   - Add a low-intensity dash reflection (probe + Fresnel).
   - Add parked wiper blades at the bottom edge; they are visible from the seat in real cabs.
8. **P2, the centre console keypad looks like a calculator** (`lezanka_*`). A 4×3 grid of identical round buttons reads as a calculator or a phone. Real switch packs use rocker or flat keys of mixed sizes with icons. Re-lay it out as 2 rows of rockers plus 1 rotary knob.
9. **P2, faceted headrest silhouette** in URP-like from the bunk (`lezanka_overcast_urp.jpg`). The headrest top shows polygon steps against the bright window. Add segments or a silhouette bevel in LOD0/1.
10. **P2, AO atlas** (`ao_atlas_preview.jpg`): about 25–30% of it is still empty white, with many sliver islands. This is acknowledged for A6.1. Pack larger islands for the large panels.
11. **P2, the render background trees** look like round broccoli on white sticks (`lezanka_overcast.jpg`, `kierowca_overcast.jpg`). The world-c v2 pines are much better. The trees are background only, but they bias every judgement of the frame.

### Keep
- The R46 eye point and the cluster typography (Inter, Polish units).
- The sightline test, the LOD pipeline with hidden-face removal, and the export check.
- The mid-tone material table.
- The cab-irradiance bake insight: README §"Najważniejszy wniosek" is correct and important.

## 3. World tile C v2 (owner: `world-builder`; lighting: `rendering-perf`)

**Verdict: FAIL.** This is a major improvement, now held back by the buildings, the empty yards and the shadow colour at low sun. Scores: geometry/topology 8 · scale cues 7 · materials 5 · vegetation 6 · buildings 3 · props/life 3 · Polish realism 6 · lighting 4 (low sun) / 6 (overcast) · artefacts 6.

### Fixed since R1 (verified)
- **Trees** are a mix of species with tapered trunks (`dk8_dk19_exit_*`).
- **Ground** now has yards, paths, fences and red bike paths.
- **DK8** has a steel median barrier, and the bridges have a galvanised grey railing.
- **Andersa** has acoustic screens (`putry_above_*`).
- **Kerbs** are grey and jointed. Crossings now connect to paths. Grass edges are no longer torn (`biezenstwa_above_*`).
- **Viaduct** casts a deck shadow (`putry_above__lowsun`).
- **Docks** (`stretch_05_docks_*`) are the best world asset so far: numbered doors, seals with yellow strips, bumpers, levellers, wheel guides and a cut concrete apron.

### Defects (ranked)
1. **P0, buildings are LoD1 boxes with punched windows** (`stretch_01/02/03_*`, `kwadrat_park_gate_*`, `putry_above_*`).
   - At driver eye level they fill 30–50% of the frame. This is the strongest remaining "2012 game" cue.
   - Halls and offices: beige render with an identical window grid; no plinth, no parapet flashing, no gutters or downpipes, no entrances or canopies, no signage.
   - The 10-storey block has no panel joints, no balconies or loggias, and none of the colour bands that come with insulation retrofits (termomodernizacja).
   - Houses are white cubes with red caps.
   - The AI streetscape and material sheets (AI reference, not photo) show what halls are missing: a concrete plinth band ≈0,5–1,0 m high, a darker parapet band, and vertical trapezoid sheet with visible fixings and downpipes.
   - **Fix:** build a facade kit for each building type:
     - hall: plinth, trapezoid sheet, parapet, downpipes, doors and canopy, plus roof vents and skylights for aerial frames;
     - office: ribbon windows, entrance canopy, fictional signage;
     - prefab panel block (wielka płyta): panel grid, loggias, insulation colour fields;
     - house: eaves, window frames, fence.
2. **P0, the world is empty.**
   - There are no parked cars, trailers, containers, pallets, bins or traffic on any frame.
   - The 174 k m² of yards are empty plains of paving blocks (`stretch_01/02/03`). A Polish logistics park at any hour has trailers at the docks, cars in staff parking and pallets beside the halls.
   - **Fix:** scatter static "life" props by land use: trailers at ramps, cars in `amenity=parking`, pallet stacks, skips, bins. Make it deterministic and GPU-instanced, with fictional liveries only (no real logos).
3. **P1 [PIPELINE], blue shadows and orange light at low sun (teal-orange)** (`putry_above__lowsun`, `stretch_01__lowsun`, `stretch_04__lowsun`, `driver_andersa_to_putry__lowsun`).
   - Every shadowed wall and kerb face turns saturated blue/teal, while the lit side goes orange. This is an explicit docs/06 §2 red flag. The "bluish median" from R1 was the same problem.
   - A Polish November low sun gives cool-grey shadows, not cyan.
   - **Fix:** desaturate the sky-irradiance term to ~50–60% of the scattering result, add a ground-bounce term, and check the white balance.
   - The new single-scattering sky (`SkyModel.cs`) must be checked for the same bias in URP.
   - Owners: `rendering-perf`, plus `world-builder` for the Blender preset.
4. **P1, the gate has no gate** (`kwadrat_park_gate_*`, `stretch_03_*`).
   - The barrier (szlaban) is a white bar on a white post. There is no gatehouse, no sliding gate, no fence returns and no sign board.
   - The AI streetscape (AI reference, not photo) shows the usual setup: a glazed gatehouse, a sliding gate, a panel fence and a red/white striped barrier arm with its housing.
   - The Z-shaped kerb notch is still visible (acknowledged in the README).
   - **Fix:** an authored gate kit for depot entrances, and a chamfered kerb transition where the carriageway splits.
5. **P1, asphalt damage looks synthetic** (foreground of `driver_andersa_to_putry_*`, `kwadrat_park_gate`).
   - Cracks are thin, ruler-straight black polylines with sharp kinks.
   - Patches form a visible regular grid of rectangles (`stretch_04__lowsun`, foreground).
   - Oil spots sit at regular spacing in the lane centres, even on free-flowing DK8, where they do not occur.
   - In the material sheet (AI reference, not photo), cracks meander and branch, and are filled with 2–4 cm glossy bitumen bands.
   - **Fix:**
     - noise-warped crack paths of variable width, with sealant bands;
     - patches with jittered size, rotation and age (colour and aggregate), 0–3 per 50 m, not on a grid;
     - oil only at stop lines, gates and parking bays.
6. **P1, monotonous, visibly tiling paving blocks** (`kwadrat_park_gate`, `stretch_01–03`).
   - Every yard uses the same rectangular block laid in a running bond, with stain blotches repeating at a fixed spacing.
   - Polish industrial yards mostly use interlocking I-shaped blocks (behaton), or plain concrete or asphalt where trucks drive.
   - **Fix:** behaton for truck yards and rectangular blocks for footways. Add a second-scale variation mask, darker tyre tracks, settlement, and weeds at the edges.
7. **P1, pines look like cedars or palms at distance** (`driver_andersa_to_putry_*` x≈150 px, `stretch_04_*` x≈520 px).
   - Their crowns are regular horizontal tiers, or a tuft on a bare pole. A mature Scots pine has an irregular, flattened, asymmetric crown with sparse clumps of foliage.
   - Some birches are bare white trunks with no crown.
   - The orange upper bark is too saturated on the DK8 right-hand stand.
   - **Fix:** irregular crown clumping, random missing branches, upper bark desaturated by ~30%, and a crown on every birch at LOD0/1.
8. **P1, grass tufts look like polka dots** (island in `driver_andersa_to_putry`, verge in `dk8_dk19_exit`).
   - They are evenly spaced, same-sized yellow-green tufts on flat green, so the scatter is obvious.
   - **Fix:** density clustered by noise × the mowing mask, size growing towards the kerbs, and some dead brown tufts.
   - The README notes this is render-only. The URP detail-grass plan must not repeat it.
9. **P1, a cross-section leftover on DK8** (`dk8_dk19_exit_*`).
   - The right edge has a raised urban kerb, because a bike path runs within 7 m.
   - At 70 km/h and above, the road edge should stay without a kerb (edge strip + verge), with the verge separating the bike path.
   - The embankment barrier rule found zero segments. Verify it against the NMT terrain model.
10. **P2, signs and lights.**
    - Placement of D-6, B-33, A-7 and C-12 looks correct.
    - Every lamp is the same 8–10 m single-arm model. Add a second family: an older steel lamp with a sodium head for side streets.
    - The pole base plinth is fine; it matches the AI reference sheet.
11. **P2, dock realism:** add black tyre-scrub arcs in front of each dock, dock lights, and dirt on the lower door panels.
12. **[PIPELINE] P1, budget.**
    - Triangle counts exceed the A16 hypothesis of 400–600 k: 8.1 M on High in the DK8 forest, and 0.81 M on Low at Andersa.
    - The A16 already uses 790 MB PSS with v1.
