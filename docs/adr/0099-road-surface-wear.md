## Deliberate rejection of broader changes

The three-block polish/gutter/longitudinal-paint experiment used 56 fresh BEFORE and 56 AFTER images. Native bindings and projected road fields proved it was active, yet actual wheel-mask changes were only 0.047-0.278 display-luma levels on old road and 0.100-0.333 on fresh road. ROOT and independent High review rejected it because it did not demonstrate a convincing improvement. Only those three blocks were restored from the exact pre-probe shader. The rejected shader, patch, pictures and metrics remain archived.

A separate packed-mask experiment repeated the existing G/B/A data byte-exactly and stored existing open-crack R at its original 16 m period. GPU import/readback confirmed 2048 ASTC 8x8, 12 mips, linear alpha, and nonzero cross-channel compression drift. A read-only render callback confirmed that Low actually used the QA shader on 44 renderers with the detail keyword disabled, mask UV multiplier 0.25, correct texture, original strength and positive R coverage. The weak result was therefore not an inactive branch. ROOT rejected the extra 1.333374 MiB shipping cost for insufficient Low benefit and High drift risk. No production material or shader was changed by that trial. Private PNG/readback data and the inactive recipe remain research only.

Low still skips the existing crack and photo-detail layers through its detail-tier gate. The 4 m scan base intentionally avoids distinctive large cracks. Adjusting polish cannot substitute for the missing evaluated feature. The existing crack network is authored procedural working data, not surveyed damage. Reviewed photographs support localized repair seams and readable worn paint; they do not justify blanket grime or an invented dense network everywhere.

Follow-up remains upstream: define reference-backed localized wear with an explicit Low texture/sampling/device budget, protect fresh-road controls and metric scale, then demonstrate a useful visible result. Do not re-enable a rejected atlas, increase amplitude blindly or call the remaining sterility solved. Existing patches/cracks/signalled oil are inventoried; their presence alone is not a quality verdict.

## Serialization and process limits

Unity serialized two signal materials by removing only their emission keyword during first capture startup. Exact initial private bytes were verified and replayed in the same condition order. START and END states are separately pinned; END AFTER matches END BEFORE for all 2441 cached files. This is an explicit exception to continuous byte stability, not a hidden shader effect. Actual instanced signal material/shader/keyword data is recorded; no lamp ROI is claimed where a closeup excludes the head.

Some captures returned the known Mono cleanup error after all fresh images and restoration. Raw codes and completion checks remain recorded; these launches are not called ordinary exit zero. Final native audit and full editor tests returned zero. Temporary material clones, render assignments and selected globals were restored in finally. The production shader stays byte-exact and private QA assets are unbound. No main merge/push, phone/ADB action or Fab derivative commit belongs to this stream.
