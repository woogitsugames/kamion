# ADR 0017 — Audio: every layer prerendered in an exterior and a cabin perspective
Status: Accepted (proposed by audio agent), 2026-11-09
Context: Interior vs exterior was solved only for the engine (ADR 0007: cab bank). All other layers (tyres, wind, pneumatics, retarder, compressor, ambience) were one clip scaled by a cab gain factor, so the cabin sounded like a quieter exterior.
Decision: `tools/audio/sfx_layers.cab_filter` renders a cabin variant of every layer offline (profiles pneu / road / wind / mech / ambient / dash: low-pass, high shelf, panel resonance at 128 Hz, floor boom). The game plays both through `PerspectiveLoop` (two AudioSources, equal-power by `InteriorBlend`; the silent one is virtualised by Unity, so no extra DSP or voices in steady state) and picks the clip for one-shots when they start. Chosen over a runtime `AudioLowPassFilter` because the prerendered filter can be arbitrarily complex and costs nothing on the Helio G99; price is about 1.5 MB more ADPCM.
Consequences: cab clips share the exterior gain (relative attenuation of the filter is kept), runtime cab factors only model insulation. Sparrow/tit have no cab clip (3-5 kHz is inaudible through closed glass). Surface loops are rendered at equal RMS; per-surface loudness lives in `LayerMath.SurfaceLevel` (WORKING_VALUE, owner listening test).
