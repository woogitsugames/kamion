# ADR 0064 - Baked ground sky visibility + far ground sun shadows (instead of lightmaps / APV)

Status: proposed (rendering-perf), 2026-11-10. Phone verification **UNVERIFIED** (no phone used: PHONE_RUN lock / lead).
Answers: "the world looks flat / gamey" (no indirect occlusion, no contact darkening; `ShaderVariantStripper` strips all
lightmap keywords) and the S24+ resolution-independent GPU cost (review r8 §0.3, ADR 0062: ≈ 13 ms of fixed passes).
Related: ADR 0010 (sky / exposure, D23), 0012 (content tiers), 0050 (parked-vehicle contact AO), 0062 (High scale floor).
Evidence: `artifacts/bake-r8/` (STATUS.md, maps, renders before/after, montages, numbers).

## Context
- World surfaces (`Kamion/World PBR`, `Kamion/World Surface`) are lit by the sun (realtime cascades: Low 35 m / 1 cascade,
  Medium 60 m, High 100 m, Ultra 150 m) and an **unoccluded** L2 SH sky. Ground at a hall wall, under a tree, under the
  DK8 overpass, between parked trailers or next to a hedge gets the full sky: no contact darkening, everything reads as
  evenly lit. Beyond the shadow distance buildings cast nothing (Low: halls 40 m away have no shadow at low sun).
- The world is static, generated, 1:1 and big (bialystok-industrial-c alone: 2048 × 2048 m, 64 chunks, 6.4 M triangles in
  the High content, 122 k instanced trees). Everything must be regenerated by code (CLAUDE.md rule 7). Lighting presets
  are fixed (LP_Clear 42°/200°, LP_LowSun 9°/60°, LP_Overcast, LP_Dusk), time of day later.

## Decision
1. **Bake** (`game/Assets/_Project/World/Import/WorldSkyBake.cs`, math in
   `Rendering/Editor/GroundSkyBakeMath.cs`), run at the end of `RegionImporter.ImportBatch`
   (`scripts/import-region.ps1`) or alone: `-executeMethod Game.World.Import.WorldSkyBake.BakeBatch -region <id>`.
   - Rasterise the High content scene into 1 m grids in region-root XZ: **ground** = lowest walkable surface (terrain,
     road, paved; triangles steeper than 73° skipped - skirts), **solid top** (buildings LOD0 or LOD1, bridges, furniture
     except see-through or thin parts: fences, rails, glass, poles, lamps, signs; precast concrete fences count), **crowns**
     (instanced trees: ellipsoid from the LOD0 bounds, bottom at 35 % height; shrubs and hedges from the ground).
     Parked vehicles are **not** occluders: ADR 0050 already darkens under them (no double AO). 1100 l bins and bus
     shelters are.
   - R: cosine-weighted sky visibility at ground + 0.15 m: 16 azimuths, horizon marched to 120 m, `mean cos²(h)`;
     crowns pass `CrownSkyTransmittance` 0.3 of the sky they cover (WORKING_VALUE), a point under a crown is multiplied
     again for the zenith part.
   - G, B: ground sun visibility for LP_LowSun and LP_Clear (ray march to 300 m, crowns `exp(-0.35 · path)`,
     WORKING_VALUE), 3×3 box (penumbra ≈ 1-2 m). Dusk / overcast: no baked sun.
   - A: ground height, 8 bit, `base + A · step` (step ≈ 0.3 m for this region).
   - Output: `WorldGI_1m.asset` on `WorldC_High`, `WorldGI_2m.asset` (2×2 mean) on `WorldC` (Low + Medium), RGBA32
     linear + mips, gitignored with the rest of `_Generated`; component `Game.Rendering.WorldSkyVisibility` on the region
     root writes the globals before every frame (follows floating-origin shifts and preset switches; no allocation).
2. **Shading** (`Rendering/Shaders/KamionWorldGI.hlsl`, included by World PBR and World Surface):
   - up-facing surfaces less than 1.5 m above the baked ground (roads, kerbs, aprons, terrain) read their own texel;
     roofs and decks (> 3 m) are untouched; walls read the ground 1.5 texels in front, normalised by the 0.5 a lone wall's
     foot sees, fading to 1 over 6 m of height;
   - ambient SH **and** environment reflection × `vis + (1 - vis) · 0.3` (occluders return 30 % of the sky radiance they
     hide - WORKING_VALUE, the open-yard asphalt keeps D23);
   - sun: `min(realtime, lerp(1, baked, GetMainLightShadowFade))` - the baked ground shadow takes over exactly where URP
     fades the cascades out, so Low gets hall shadows beyond 35 m and High beyond 100 m.
3. **Switches** (every tier on by default; inverted fields so tiers saved before this ADR stay on):
   `QualityProfile.Tier.worldSkyVisOff`, `worldBakedSunOff`; launch args `rq_worldgi 0|1`, `rq_worldsun 0|1`
   (also `-Rq` of `scripts/visual-qa.ps1`). No component (DrivingLab, Boot) = strength 0 = no effect.
4. **No change** to `ShaderVariantStripper` (lightmap keywords stay stripped - nothing here uses them), ProjectSettings,
   asmdefs, URP assets, materials, textures of surfaces (ADR 0063), vegetation shading (ADR 0060), glass, render scale
   (ADR 0062).

## Consequences / open
- Phone: A16 Low and S24+ High A/B `rq_worldgi 0` vs default (GPU p50/p90, PSS). Expected cost < 0.3 ms on the A16
  (one cache-coherent fetch: a 2 m texel covers many pixels) - UNVERIFIED.
- Cost reduction on High (r8 / ADR 0062 fixed passes): with far ground shadows baked, the High shadow distance can drop
  100 → 60-70 m (fewer casters per cascade, smaller cascade 3) - A/B `rq_shadow 60` on the S24+; not changed here.
- Vegetation (grass cards, hedge shells, impostors) does not read the map yet (ADR 0060 owner): grass under a tree
  stays bright while the soil under it darkens.
- Moving vehicles / the player's truck are not occluders (realtime + ADR 0021 / 0050 do that).
- Time of day: the R channel is preset-independent; the sun channels need one channel per baked sun direction (or a
  horizon-angle map, 8 directions, evaluated per pixel) when the sun moves.
- 1 m RGBA32 on High = 21.3 MB; ASTC 4×4 would be 5.3 MB but breaks the 8-bit height channel (needs a separate R16 or
  a smaller base grid) - only if the S24+ PSS needs it.
