# F2 capture preflight: available evidence and a bounded causal packet

Read-only preparation for ADR 0115, 2026-11-11. F remains closed at 2086e780 with explicit appearance failures. This note does not implement F2 or change D. Main source was inspected at 7265bd65; exact file hashes accompany this note. ROOT's current phone hold still prohibits Unity. D's 16 frames have priority after release.

## What the saved data actually establishes

- `candidate1-door-actual-shader-fields.json`: historical door medians are coat 1, AO 1, Fresnel factor about 0.051, perceptual roughness 0.0976 and reflected direction Y about -0.030. The sampled environment varies down the door: High Y 0.354 / 0.289 / 0.143 and Low 0.210 / 0.176 / 0.131 at top / middle / bottom. This excludes a disabled coat or uniformly zero environment in that old fixture. These diagnostic-derived fields are historical, not a fresh full-resolution HDR buffer or a measurement of current main.
- `candidate1-env-directions/High-overcast/environment-directions.json`: actual GPU red-channel samples are 1.0625 upward and 0.11310 downward, and 1.00488 versus 0.13464 for the saved +/-Y diagonal pair. Thus the old cube contains a sky/ground distinction; a completely missing dark ground hemisphere is unsupported. These are red-channel values for specific rays, not full RGB luminance at the actual door's narrow horizon rays.
- The saved `before-coat-radiance-off/High-overcast/truck-34_envcube.png` was inspected. Real ground, paving, buildings and sky are present. `VehicleReflectionCapture.DumpStrip` copies Half values, applies `Color.gamma`, then writes RGB24 PNG; highlights clip. No saved EXR/HDR/NPY cube was found in this packet. A PNG cannot reconstruct unclipped sky radiance, exposure calibration or the exact narrow horizon contrast. Face-strip orientation must follow the recorded face rotations, not an assumption from its display layout. Prior dominant-horizontal orientation tests passed; repeating a flip hypothesis is unwarranted without new evidence.
- `paint-color-probe.json`: historical GPU upload of authored Color 0.78 yields 0.57048, and 0.785 yields 0.57871. The current label still says linear, but these legacy setter values are not physical linear reflectance. Preserve that distinction in any diagnostic; do not silently change the production contract.
- `candidate3-final-coat-contribution.json`: isolated radiance-only coat removal, with base roughness and attenuation preserved, changes the old overcast door by +1.28 Low / +1.66 High display percentage points. Coat is present but visually weak. The final owner profile remains below threshold. The 3.6 mm crown affects both diffuse and coat, so its full image delta is not a pure coat measurement.
- `candidate1-capture-resolution-control.json`: High 128 versus 256 gives glass median 28.908 versus 28.936 and sigma 11.113 versus 11.074 in the recorded crop. This does not justify raising default capture resolution. The later pane-off controls also classified the hard upper silhouette as transmitted geometry, not proof of a cube pixel step.

## Current source and the remaining hypothesis

Main retains the door-only object-space 3.6 mm crown, mapped normals, F0 0.04, dust attenuation and coherent diffuse/coat normal. `KamionVehicleEnv` maps box-filtered cube mip from alpha = roughness squared and actual face size; the analytic fallback remains separate. `VehicleReflectionCapture` uses ARGBHalf, excludes vehicle/UI layers, and renders with the display tone vector zero and the physical-sky flag set, restoring prior globals in `finally`. No missing coat, normal flip or absent ground hemisphere is established by this inspection.

The supported hypothesis is narrower: the actual door rays occupy only a small band around the horizon, and their nonzero reflected contrast may be small compared with the bright pigment's diffuse output and final white rolloff. Saved fields support testing this; they do not prove whether the current captured local horizon is too weak or whether its signal is merely obscured. The old sparse red rays and clipped PNG faces cannot decide that question. No F0, crown amplitude, environment gain or production pigment change follows from the evidence.

## Small future packet, one variable per comparison

1. Establish a fresh current-main canonical fixture, final normal import, camera and vehicle runtime poses, actual keyword/tier and capture size/LOD/mip. Keep C's exposure/shoulder fixed. Use the same door mask in `truck-34` / `truck-side`, roof mask in High-clear `truck-34r`, and pane masks in `truck-front`. Never compare the old F baseline against a new merged fixture as though only paint changed.
2. Save actual cube mip 0 and sampled mip as unclipped RGB Half/float data, plus capture globals and face matrices. At the actual door rays, read R, mip, raw environment, horizon-occlusion factor, Fresnel, pigment BRDF, `env * Fc` and direct coat independently. A transient shader diagnostic must output those values before the shader's own `KamionTone`; a final camera HDR texture may already contain that tone operation. Save pre-tone and final display values separately, with matching masks.
3. Run a four-case diagnostic on the same frozen cube: legacy white / temporary dark pigment, each with coat ON / radiance-only coat OFF. Use the already validated radiance-only removal, preserving pigment roughness and coat attenuation. Verify actual GPU pigment upload for both colors. Dark pigment is a causal control, never the production white-paint remedy. If reflected spatial contrast survives in raw coat and becomes visible only against dark pigment, white diffuse dominance is supported. If raw coat is already flat at the relevant rays, inspect local captured content instead.
4. Only if those sampled rays identify a deficient local horizon: one QA-only cube-content control, with the rest of the cube, roughness, F0, pigment and exposure frozen. Preserve a copy of the real cube and log the exact altered angular band. An explicit two-band horizon diagnostic can test sensitivity; it is synthetic evidence, not permission to ship a sky-only substitute for real ground/buildings. If the real cube already has adequate local contrast, skip this control and investigate the measured downstream term or rolloff instead.
5. Glass: compare pane upper/lower thirds and interior legibility on current Low, Medium and High. `FragFast` receives vertex transmittance `exp(-tau)` already; a future premultiplied in-scatter fix must weight haze by output alpha and keep that transmittance, not apply another exponential. Check its capture roughness separately from the full path. Preserve the fast-path sampling/pass policy; source arithmetic cost and A16 time remain unverified until measured.
6. DRL: keep the finite 1000-nits-per-encoded-unit scheme. Current `DrlNits` is 160000, a working value. Measure the same lamp mask's clipped fraction, p95 and lengthwise profile on current High/Low clear/overcast and a selected low-sun control before one bounded daytime calibration. Preserve OFF, automatic day/night and low-beam dimming controls. The critic's historical 1129 clipped pixels are a starting observation, not a fresh result.

Temporary materials, diagnostic shaders and global/cube bindings must restore exact prior state in `finally`; hash production shaders and capture globals before/after. No new default capture faces, samples, materials or runtime passes are proposed here. Renderer-counter parity does not establish GPU cost. Any shader arithmetic/fast-glass change needs matched counters and the leader's device measurement. This preflight made no source, generated-product, Unity or phone changes.
